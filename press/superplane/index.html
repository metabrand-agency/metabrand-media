SuperPlane Brand Identity and Generative Art

SuperPlane is an AI software factory. Agents take routine engineering tickets from issue to a review-ready pull request, and engineers step in only where judgment is needed. The company is built by the team behind Semaphore, the CI/CD platform used by Confluent, Replit and Superhuman, and raised a $2.6M pre-seed round in 2026.

Metabrand built the brand on one idea: every engineer and agent is a vector, and company progress is their sum. Agents add speed. SuperPlane gives them direction, so the work adds up instead of canceling out.

The arrow became the one element the identity needs. In the copy it works as a verb, with headlines written as input → output: Issues → PRs, Detection → Recovery. The mark comes straight from the product: two workflow cards joined by a connector that bends into an S. The palette is made for engineers who live in dark mode, with Signal Orange held back for the moment something moves. Instrument Sans carries the voice and Roboto Mono the numbers.

Metabrand also wrote a generative engine for the brand. Thousands of arrows gather into a globe, flock and follow the cursor, and the same engine renders posts, slides and event screens.
